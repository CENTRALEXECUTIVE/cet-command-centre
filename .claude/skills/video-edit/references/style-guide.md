# Style Guide — Feez
*Extracted from 4 example videos. Last updated: 2026-04-19.*
*Source videos: "different types of hooks", "if your profile looks like this you're cooked", "never post and ghost", "solo leveling method"*

---

## Format

- **Dimensions**: 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical, Reels/Shorts)
- **Frame rate**: 30fps
- **Duration range**: 26–50s (sweet spot: 28–35s)
- **Total cuts**: 10–15 per video (more cuts = more energy; fewer = more authority)

---

## Pacing

### Opening shot — hold it
The first shot is held **4–11s**. This is the hook window. Don't cut away from your face until the premise is landed.

| Video | Opening shot duration |
|---|---|
| different types of hooks | 11.0s |
| if your profile looks like this | 2.9s |
| never post and ghost | 5.9s |
| solo leveling method | 7.1s |

**Rule**: Open cut ≥ 4s. Cut shorter only if opening is pure action/b-roll (no talking).

### Mid-section — cut fast
After the hook, cuts tighten to **1.2–2.5s**. Rhythm is punchy. Remove every breath, um, pause.

Observed mid-section averages:
- "if your profile": 1.3–2.0s cuts → most energetic
- "never post and ghost": 1.2–2.3s cuts
- "solo leveling method": 1.4–2.3s cuts

**Rule**: Mid-section target = 1.5–2.5s per cut. Below 1.0s only for emphasis beats.

### Closing — land it
Final 2–3 cuts hold slightly longer (2–4s) to let the CTA breathe. Don't end on a rushed cut.

### Pacing curve
```
Open:    SLOW  (4–11s) ─── hook lands
Mid:     FAST  (1–2.5s) ── value delivered
Close:   MED   (2–4s) ──── CTA + out
```

## Music

- **Always present** — no video has zero background music
- **BPM range**: 99–123 bpm across examples (mean ~114 bpm)
- **Feel**: Upbeat but not frantic. Motivational/lo-fi-hype category
- **Volume**: Background only — speech is primary. Music at ~12–18% volume relative to speech

---

## Cuts

- **Cut style**: Hard cuts only. No dissolves, no wipes observed
- **No silence between cuts**: Music fills all gaps. Cut audio is clean (no silence regions detected in any video)
- **J-cuts** (audio before video): Likely used but not detectable without transcript review
- **B-roll**: Yes — used to cover cuts and illustrate points. Keep b-roll clips 1–3s, don't linger

## Captions (to be confirmed with transcript review)

*Observed conventions — update after manual review of example videos:*

- **Style**: Bold, centered, large — typical viral short-form caption style
- **Position**: Lower-center of frame (above bottom 15%)
- **Color**: White text, possibly with colored word emphasis on key words
- **Animation**: Pop-on per word or phrase (not letter-by-letter)
- **Casing**: ALL CAPS or Title Case — confirm from examples
- **Emphasis**: 1–2 words per segment highlighted in a different color (yellow or accent color)

---

## Entry cut (hook optimization)

The hook is already in the footage. The editor's job is finding where it really starts:
- Skip pre-roll: remove any "alright so" / "okay so" / "hey guys" pre-amble
- Find the first word that is part of the actual content
- If there's b-roll intro: cut to face at first word of value, not at first frame

---

## What NOT to do

- Don't hold on a cut longer than 4s in the mid-section (kills retention)
- Don't fade to black between scenes
- Don't leave silences/dead air
- Don't add intro/outro cards (these are Reels, not YouTube)
- Don't use music with vocals that compete with speech
