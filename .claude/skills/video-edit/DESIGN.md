# Design — viral-shortform-editor

Visual identity for all HyperFrames compositions generated by this skill.
Derived from the extracted style guide (4 reference videos).

## Style Prompt

High-energy social content. Bold, direct, no decoration. The subject is the visual — overlays exist only to reinforce the spoken word. Hard cuts, zero transitions, full-bleed footage. Typography is a weapon: massive, uppercase, maximum contrast, with one yellow word per caption group as a precision emphasis tool.

## Colors

| Role      | Hex       | Use |
|-----------|-----------|-----|
| Background | `#000000` | Stage background, letterbox |
| Text       | `#FFFFFF` | Caption base color |
| Emphasis   | `#FBBA2A` | 1–2 key words per caption group |
| Shadow     | `#000000` | Caption text-shadow (outline) |

No gradients. No additional colors. The footage IS the background.

## Typography

- **Captions:** `TikTok Sans`, `Helvetica Neue`, `Arial`, `sans-serif` — 500 weight (Medium), lowercase
- **Max 5 words per caption group** — longer captions are split and timed sequentially within the clip
- Font size: 64–72px
- TikTok Sans must be installed on the render machine (system font, not a web font)

## Motion

- Caption entrance: fast pop-up from 18px below, 0.15s, `power2.out`
- Caption exit: fade out 0.12s before clip end, `power2.in`
- No transitions between video clips — hard cuts only (style guide rule)
- No decorative animations on the video layer

## What NOT to Do

- No dissolves, wipes, or crossfades between clips
- No intro/outro cards
- No music with lyrics that compete with speech
- No caption font other than Impact/Arial Black
- No more than 2 emphasis words per caption group
- Never hold a mid-section clip longer than 4s
