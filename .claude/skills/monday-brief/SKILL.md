name: monday-brief
description: The weekly one-page business brief for Central Executive Transfers — numbers, what worked, what didn't, and the directors' 5 jobs this week ranked by money. Use when the user says /monday-brief or asks for the weekly brief.
---

1. Read `docs/growth/PLAYBOOK.md`, then the newest file in each of
   `docs/growth/{ads,sales,seo,retention,social,product}/` and any new exports in
   `docs/growth/data/` (never commit or quote personal data from them).
2. For areas with new data, ask the matching agent for a 3-line update
   (google-ads-strategist, b2b-sales-hunter, seo-local-search, reviews-retention,
   social-media-manager). Skip areas with nothing new — say "no new data".
3. Write `docs/growth/briefs/<date>.md`, one page:
   - Last week: bookings/enquiries by source if known, ad spend + cost per booking,
     new reviews, outreach sent/replies, social highlights.
   - What worked / what didn't (one line each).
   - **Your 5 jobs this week**, ranked by money impact, each doable in < 1 hour.
   - Waiting on you: open decisions and `[CONFIRM]` facts.
4. Reply with the 5 jobs and the decisions — short enough to read on a phone.
