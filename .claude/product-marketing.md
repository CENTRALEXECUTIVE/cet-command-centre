## Snapshot
- Owner-run executive chauffeur firm, Sheffield (South Yorkshire), trading since 2024.
  Directors Abdi & Maj drive the executive jobs themselves.
- Core offer: fixed-price airport transfers (Manchester, East Midlands, Leeds Bradford,
  Heathrow, Gatwick, Birmingham…), corporate accounts with VAT invoices, weddings/events, hourly hire.
- Proof points that are true and built: flight tracking, meet & greet on arrivals, live
  driver tracking link, number-masked calls, 24/7, council-licensed + enhanced-DBS drivers.

## Audience
- Sheffield-region travellers flying out/in; families with luggage (V-Class/8-seaters).
- Corporate travel bookers/PAs (JELD-WEN, LB Foster, Forged Solutions are clients).
- Wedding couples; international visitors booking an arrival before they fly.

## Voice
Calm, premium, straightforward British English. Short sentences. No hype, no emojis
spam, no "it's not X, it's Y". Sign-off for outreach: "Abdi & Maj, Directors".

## Hard rules
- Nothing is ever posted or sent automatically — drafts and files only; the directors post.
- Brand: gold `#FBBA2A`, black, white. Logo mark: the circle "C".
- No customer faces, names, plates or addresses in content without written consent.
- Free tools first (only Google Maps and Anthropic cost money) — flag any paid tool before using it.
