# CET 90-day social growth plan: 2 November 2026 to 31 January 2027

Builds on `setup.md` (scheduler, bios, link-in-bio, 15-shot evergreen list) and
`2026-11-02-week.md` (week 1 pack). Facts only from `BUSINESS_FACTS.md`. Anything
unconfirmed is marked `[CONFIRM: ...]` and collected in section 9.

Nothing here posts itself. A director schedules or posts every item. No customer
faces, names, plates, addresses or flight details without written consent. No client
named without their written permission. No fake reviews, stats or "No.1" claims.

Dates: Week 1 starts Mon 2 Nov. Day 30 = Mon 30 Nov. Day 60 = Mon 28 Dec. Day 90 = Sun 31 Jan.

### Starting position (be honest about it)

  credible when the person checks us out. Prospects will Google us and open our
  profiles before they reply to a B2B email. Empty profiles cost us those replies.

### Goal 1: Be visibly real and active (presence)

A new visitor sees a consistent, premium, active brand on every channel, with recent
posts, real photos of the cars and the directors, and a full Google profile.

### Goal 2: Be trusted (proof)

Google reviews are the single most valuable asset for a local chauffeur firm.
Directors are known by name and face on LinkedIn, so corporate bookers feel
comfortable replying.

### Goal 3: Turn attention into enquiries and corporate accounts (business)

Every channel has a tracked booking link and a "how did you hear about us" answer
logged. B2B conversations start from LinkedIn and warm outreach.

## 2. Platform roles and time split

Ranked by value to a chauffeur firm: people search for a driver at the moment they
need one (Google), and corporate bookers choose on trust (LinkedIn and reviews).

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Google Business Profile | Local conversion | People searching "airport taxi Sheffield" see the profile and map listing first. Reviews, photos and weekly posts directly lift calls and bookings. Free. Book button and call button sit where the decision happens. | 30% |
| 2 | LinkedIn (two director profiles plus company page) | Corporate accounts | The buyers of VAT-invoiced, monthly-account travel are on LinkedIn. Director profiles get far more reach than a company page. Backs up the outreach emails. | 25% |
| 3 | Instagram | Premium shop window and Reels | Where private customers and referrers judge quality: the cars, the standards, the people. Reels give reach beyond followers. | 20% |

| 5 | TikTok | Cheap experiment | Reach potential for behind-the-scenes and airport tips, but the audience skews young and the buying link is weak. Reuse Reels, post by hand, no extra filming. | 10% |

## 5. Growth levers beyond posting

### 5.1 Google reviews engine (highest priority)

Rules: ask every completed customer, never offer discounts or gifts for a review, never
write or request fake ones, never filter to only happy customers (ask everyone), reply
to every review. A director sends each message by hand (WhatsApp or text from the
office line). Nothing is automated.

Set up (Week 1): get the Google review short link from GBP ("Ask for reviews"), make a
QR of it, and keep the link in a saved WhatsApp reply. `[CONFIRM: short link]`

**When:** the same day, 1 to 3 hours after drop-off, never during the journey, never
at 03:00. Corporate and account clients: ask once, in a personal email, after the third
journey. Passengers who are not the booker: ask the booker.

**Ask script (WhatsApp/text):**

> Hello [first name], thank you for travelling with Central Executive Transfers today.
> If you were happy with the journey, a short Google review helps other people in
> Sheffield find a driver they can trust. It takes about 30 seconds: [link]
> If anything was not right, please reply here and we will put it right.
> Abdi / Maj, Central Executive Transfers

**Chase (once only, 5 days later, only if no reply):**

> Hello [first name], a gentle reminder in case it slipped by. A line or two about the
> journey is plenty: [link]. Thank you either way.

**Driver-handover line (in the car, at the end):** "If you were happy, a Google review
means a lot to a small firm like ours. I will send you the link."

**Reply to every review within 48 hours.** Template (5 stars): "Thank you, [first
name]. We are glad the journey went well. Safe travels, and we would be glad to see you
again. Abdi/Maj." Do not repeat journey details, addresses, times or flight numbers in
a public reply. For a poor review: thank, apologise for the specific problem without
arguing, move it offline ("please call 0114 399 8242"), and record the lesson. No
defensive replies.

**Targets:** ask 100% of private jobs; aim for 20 to 30% to leave a review; 8 / 18 / 30
cumulative by day 30 / 60 / 90 (section 1). Count the asks in the scorecard.

**Reuse reviews:** a review shown on social only if the reviewer's public name is
already on Google, and we ask them first. Quote the words exactly.

**Account clients and word of mouth:** the referral email in
`docs/growth/sales/referral-ask-account-clients.md` is the model. Any thank-you
for referrals is `[CONFIRM]` and must not breach a client's gift policy. Private customers: print a small card with the booking link and "Recommend us to a friend"
in each car `[CONFIRM: no incentive until the directors decide]`.
