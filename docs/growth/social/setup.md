# CET social setup

## 1. Which ONE scheduler

Checked via WebSearch on 28 Oct 2026. Sources were third-party reviews, not the vendors' own pages, so a director must confirm each limit on the pricing page before signing up.

| Tool | Free tier (as reported) | Fits CET? |
|---|---|---|
| Metricool | 1 brand; 20 scheduled posts/month (older guides say 50); all networks EXCEPT LinkedIn and X; 30 days analytics. LinkedIn needs paid Starter (about $20-25/month). | Covers Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, GBP but NOT LinkedIn, which is our B2B channel. 20 posts/month is below our plan (about 30). |
| Buffer | 3 channels; 10 queued posts per channel at a time (a slot reopens when a post publishes); 30 days analytics. Paid is about $5-6 per channel per month. | Supports LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, GBP, but only 3 at once. |
| Meta Business Suite | Free, Facebook + Instagram only. Native CSV bulk upload not confirmed. | Only 2 of our 5 channels. |

No free tier covers all five channels. This is the honest finding.

**Recommendation: Buffer free, with LinkedIn + Instagram + Facebook connected.**
- LinkedIn is the B2B channel and Metricool free cannot do it.
- Instagram and Facebook carry most of the volume.
- Buffer's queue limit (10 per channel) suits 2-3 posts per channel per week.
- GBP (2 posts/week) and TikTok (reuse of the Reel) are posted by hand in about 5 minutes each, using the same CSV rows. Alternative: pay about $5-6/month per extra channel for GBP first, since it drives local bookings. That is a director decision (cost rule: free where possible).
- Buffer does not clearly offer CSV bulk import on free. [CONFIRM] If not, copy the captions from the CSV/calendar; the CSV is still the master record. Metricool does support bulk planning, so if CSV import matters more than LinkedIn, swap to Metricool free and post LinkedIn by hand.

Account hygiene: one shared business email (admin@centralexecutivetransfers.co.uk), 2FA on every account, both directors as admins, passwords in a password manager, Instagram set to a Professional (Business) account linked to the Facebook Page.
