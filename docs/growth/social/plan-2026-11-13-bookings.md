# Bookings plan: social media and free growth (13 Nov 2026 to 31 Jan 2027)

The directors asked for a realistic plan: "we need bookings". This replaces the follower-led parts of
`90-day-growth-plan.md`. House rules still apply: no prices in posts, never mention directors driving or
"owner-run", no customer faces, names or flight details without consent, nothing invented.

## The honest picture

- Google Ads were paused on 4 Nov. Ads brought most new website customers (about 29 a month).
- **Social media alone will not replace that quickly.** A new local account typically takes 2 to 3
  months of steady posting before it brings regular enquiries.
- What social *does* do straight away is make us look real and trusted when people check us out from
  Google, outreach emails or a friend's recommendation. Empty or stale profiles lose those bookings.
- The **fastest free bookings** come from people who already know us: past customers, repeat business
  accounts, Google reviews and search, and local partners.

So the plan is in two parts. Part A is bookings in the next 30 days; Part B is building the audience.

## Part A: bookings in the next 30 days (do these first)

| # | Action | Who / how | Why it books |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Christmas and New Year message to past customers** ("airport runs over Christmas, book early") | Sent by hand on WhatsApp/email from the Command Centre customer list, 10 to 20 a day | Past customers book again fastest. December is peak airport season. |
| 2 | **Google reviews from every finished job** | Already automatic invite after completion; directors also ask in person | Reviews are the number-one free driver of local search bookings. |
| 3 | **Google Business Profile post every week** with a real photo and a "Book" button | Directors, 5 minutes, using this pack's photos | Shows in Maps and search, where people actually look for "airport transfer Sheffield". |
| 4 | **Corporate outreach, 10 a day, businesses first** | Already running from the Command Centre | One account (like Forged Solutions) is worth dozens of one-off fares. |
| 5 | **Hotels and venues as referrers** (Sheffield hotels' concierge desks, Fox Valley, the Cathedral, wedding venues) | A short visit or email with a card; offer a smooth booking line for their guests | They recommend a car to guests every week. |
| 6 | **Christmas party and New Year's Eve groups** (V-Class for 7) | One post plus outreach to venues hosting parties | High-value bookings on dates customers plan ahead. |
| 7 | **Ask every happy customer for a referral** ("know someone flying soon?") | Directors on the day; the tip/thanks page already invites a review | Word of mouth is free and converts best. |

## Part B: building the audience (followers who later book)

### Where to post

| Platform | What | How often |
|---|---|---|
| **TikTok** | The Reels (prep, fleet, weddings, airport runs) with a trending sound added in the app | 3 a week |
| **Instagram** | The same Reels, plus photo carousels and Stories | 3 Reels a week, Stories most days |
| **Facebook** | The same Reels and photos, plus Sheffield community groups | 3 a week |
| **LinkedIn** | Business travel, accounts, VAT invoices, airport reliability (company page) | 1 to 2 a week |
| **Google Business Profile** | Photo plus short update | 1 a week |

Film once, post everywhere: one video goes to TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.

### What to post (content pillars)

1. **Behind the scenes:** car wash, prep, water bottles, ready to roll (the prep Reel).
2. **The cars:** E-Class, V-Class, Rolls-Royce on request (the fleet Reel).
3. **Airport runs:** arrivals, meet and greet board, flight tracking (filmed safely, never while driving).
4. **Weddings and events:** ribbons, matching cars, the Cathedral (the wedding Reel).
5. **Trust:** licensed chauffeurs, live tracking link, fixed fares, VAT invoices for businesses.
6. **Sheffield:** local landmarks, the city at night, Fox Valley, the Cathedral.

### Every week (about 2 hours in total)

- Film 15 minutes of clips on normal jobs: car wash, interior, airport arrivals, the city. 0.5x lens, 60fps.
- Send them to Claude: they come back as finished Reels in the chosen style, music-free.
- Post 3 Reels (add a trending sound in the app), 1 GBP post, 1 LinkedIn post.
- 15 minutes a day replying to comments and commenting on local Sheffield, wedding and business accounts.
- Look at the **Bookings by source** report on Monday: which "How did you hear about us?" answers grew.
