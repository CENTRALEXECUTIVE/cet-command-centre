## What it means

1. **We aren't the cheapest to Manchester** (£100 vs £90 after discount). Don't fight on price
   alone, and don't say "cheap". Show a **clear fixed price plus the extras the £90 firms
   don't mention.** Once D2 confirms the price, "Manchester Airport from £100" next to
   "Flight Tracking Included" beats a bare £90.
2. **Gaps nobody else claims (all true for CET, all in BUSINESS_FACTS):**
   - **Live driver tracking link**
   - **Number-masked calls** (privacy)
   - **Directors drive the executive jobs** (owner-run, accountable)
   - **VAT invoices + monthly invoicing** for business
   - **Card, payment link or cash**

   These are in every RSA.
3. **Free waiting time and free cancellation** are what the aggregators sell hardest
   (Transfeero: 60 min free waiting). If CET offers them, confirm (D3) and they become
   headlines: "60 Mins Free Waiting" (20 chars), "Free Cancellation" (17).
4. **Aggregators price per person** (£15–£26 pp). Possible line for 4 passengers:
   "£25 a Head for Four to MAN". Only if D2 confirms £100, and it must say "for four".
5. **Exec/chauffeur competitors sell the car** (S-Class, Bentley). BUSINESS_FACTS forbids
   promising a model, so we sell **who drives and how it's run**: directors, fixed prices,
   tracking, invoices. Hourly pricing (£45–£80/h locally) is a gap on our side. Decision:
   publish an hourly "as directed" rate? (The booking widget already has an hourly-hire option.)
6. **Credentials.** Izec leads with ROSPA/Guild. Our equivalent would be "Licensed by
   Sheffield City Council", "DBS-checked" and "5.0 Google rating". All three are on our site
   but `[CONFIRM]` in BUSINESS_FACTS (D3). Confirm them and they go straight into the ads.
