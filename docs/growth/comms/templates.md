# Reply template library — Central Executive Transfers

Starter library of 20 situations. Each has a WhatsApp version and an email version.
Nothing here sends itself: a director reads, fills the gaps and sends.

## How to use

- `{curly braces}` = fill in. `[PRICE NEEDED]`, `[CONFIRM: …]` = a director must supply it before sending.
- Use only confirmed prices. Never invent one. Instant price is at https://centralexecutivetransfers.co.uk
- Times are UK local. Repeat date, time, pickup, drop-off, passengers, bags, vehicle and flight number back so errors get caught.
- Never promise a specific car or driver. Never share a driver's real number (masked line only). Never confirm a booking that is not in the system.
- Drivers: first name only to customers.
- Complaints: acknowledge, take responsibility where due, no excuses, no blame, offer a concrete fix, move to a call. Never argue in writing.
- Do not name corporate clients publicly.
- Saved approved replies get added under the right scenario with a date. None yet.
