## Before sending - checklist

1. **Find the named role** (PA/EA, office or facilities manager, travel coordinator,
   procurement) on the company website or LinkedIn. Use a **published business
   address** or the company's contact route. Don't guess personal addresses, and
   don't email sole traders without consent.
2. Replace `[Name]`. If no name is public, send to the role-based address and open with "Hello,".
3. Sign as **Abdi** or **Maj**. Whoever sends owns the follow-ups.
4. Keep the opt-out line. If someone opts out, mark them `opted-out` in `PIPELINE.md` and never contact them again.
5. Every claim below comes from `BUSINESS_FACTS.md`. Do not add insurance, DBS
   vetting, 24/7 cover, years trading or client names until those are confirmed.
