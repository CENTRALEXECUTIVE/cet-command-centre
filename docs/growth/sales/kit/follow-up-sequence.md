# Follow-up sequence (corporate, Track A)

Drafts only. **Maj or Abdi sends every message**, from their own mailbox, in plain
text. The person who sent email 1 sends all of the follow-ups.

This kit replaces the generic corporate follow-ups and the day-21 close in
`../2026-10-28-emails.md`. The partner (Track B) day-4 and day-10 emails in that
file still stand, and there are extra partner variants at the end of this file.

| Day | Touch | Channel | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Email 1 | Email | From the batch file. Touch 1. |
| 4 | Follow-up 1 | Email, same thread | New angle. Touch 2. |
| 5 to 9 | One call | Phone | Optional. See `phone-script.md`. |
| 10 | Follow-up 2 | Email, new subject | Short, with something useful. Touch 3. |
| 10 to 11 | Connect | LinkedIn | Only if you can find the right person. |
| 18 | Close the loop | Email | Then set to closed (no reply) for six months. |

**Stop rule:** after three unanswered touches, the next message is the close.
If they reply at any point, drop the sequence and answer them properly.

**Standard signature (plain text, no logo)**

```
Maj
Director, Central Executive Transfers Ltd
0114 399 8242 · admin@centralexecutivetransfers.co.uk
centralexecutivetransfers.co.uk · Licensed operator OP037 · Company No. 15749931
```
[CONFIRM: Maj's direct email address for the signature, if it's not admin@.]

**Opt-out line (every email, under the signature)**

> If this isn't relevant, just reply "no thanks" and I won't contact you again.

Personalise the **first line** of every follow-up. Use something real: a new
contract, a job advert, an event, a site visit in the news. If there's nothing
new, refer back to what you said in email 1.

If it's useful, I can set up an account and you can try us on your next visitor. That one journey would be invoiced exactly like the rest.

Worth a 10-minute call this week?

[Signature]

## LinkedIn connection note (300 characters maximum)

person, if you emailed a role address). No pitch in the note. Character counts
below are with the placeholders as written. Check the count again after you fill
in the name and company.

## Warm referral: first email

Use this only when the person really did suggest it **and** has agreed to be
named. If the referrer works for one of our account clients, naming them still
needs their OK. Ask: "Is it all right if I say you suggested it?" Never say what
work we do for the referrer's company unless they've agreed to that too.

### Variant A
**Subject:** [Referrer first name] at [Referrer company] suggested I get in touch

Hello [Name],

[Referrer full name] at [Referrer company] suggested I get in touch. [He/She] mentioned you [look after visitor travel / book airport transfers for the leadership team / are setting up the new site] and thought we might be useful.

I'm one of the two directors of Central Executive Transfers, a licensed chauffeur firm in Sheffield (operator licence OP037). We work on account for businesses across South Yorkshire. Prices are fixed in advance and you get driver details and a live tracking link before every pickup. Airport pickups come with flight tracking and meet and greet, and every journey goes on one monthly VAT invoice from our VAT-registered Ltd.

Would a 10-minute call this week or next be useful?

[Signature]
[Opt-out line]

*(About 115 words.)*

**Subject:** Introduction from [Referrer first name]

Hello [Name],

[Referrer first name] at [Referrer company] passed on your name and said you might need a reliable car service for [visitors / the team / candidates].

I'm Maj, a director at Central Executive Transfers in Sheffield. We're small and owner-run, and the directors drive the executive jobs themselves, so you'd always be dealing with the people responsible. We offer fixed prices, flight tracking and tracking links, with one VAT invoice a month.

Could I call you for 10 minutes, or would you rather I sent the rate card?

[Signature]
[Opt-out line]
