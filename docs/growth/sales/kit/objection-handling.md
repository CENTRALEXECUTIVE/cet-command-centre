# Objection handling

Short, honest answers that use only facts from `../../BUSINESS_FACTS.md` and what
the Command Centre actually does. **If an answer depends on a `[CONFIRM]`, don't
promise it. Say "I'll confirm that in writing today" and then do.**

House rules:
- Never knock another firm by name. Say what we do, not what they don't.
- Never name an existing client. Say "South Yorkshire manufacturers and engineering
  firms already use us on account."
- Don't claim insurance details, DBS vetting, 24/7 cover, years trading or journey
  counts until the directors confirm them.
- If a "no" is a real no, accept it, log it, and stop.

---

### 1. "We use Uber / Addison Lee / a local taxi firm."
"That's fine, and we're not asking you to switch everything. You could keep them
for what they do well and use us for visitor and airport work from South
Yorkshire. With us the price is agreed before the journey, the flight is tracked,
and you get the driver's details and a tracking link before pickup. It all goes on
one VAT invoice a month. Shall I send rates for your two or three most common routes?"

### 2. "We have a travel agent / travel management company."
"Good. We're happy to work behind them as the local supplier, so they keep the
booking and we do the driving in South Yorkshire. If you book some journeys direct,
such as a visitor from the station or a short-notice airport run, those can go on
your own account with us. Which TMC do you use? I'll contact them as well, if you're
happy for me to."

### 3. "Just send me your prices."
"Of course. To make it useful rather than a generic list, which two or three routes
do you use most, and is it usually one or two people or a group?" Then send a
specific quote for those routes the same day. Until the corporate rate card is
approved, don't attach a full card. Quote only the routes they asked for, from the
Pricing matrix, after a director has checked the figures.

### 4. "We don't travel much."
"That's fine. There's no minimum [CONFIRM: no minimum spend, no account fee]. Most
of the value is in the few journeys that matter, like the senior visitor, the
customer audit or the late flight. With an account set up, booking one is a phone
call, and it turns up on the monthly invoice."

### 5. "You're too expensive."
"We're not the cheapest way to get from A to B, and we don't pretend to be. The
price is fixed when you book, with no surge. It includes flight tracking and
driver details with a tracking link. For airport arrivals, meet and greet is
[CONFIRM: included on account / £10 as on the public price]. If it helps, give me a
recent journey and what you paid, and I'll show you our like-for-like price."
Don't discount on the call. Take it back to the directors (see the rate card's
account-discount options).

### 6. "We can only use approved suppliers / it has to go through procurement."
"Understood. Who should I contact to get on the list? I'll fill in the supplier
forms. You'll have our company number (15749931), VAT number [CONFIRM], operator
licence (OP037, Sheffield City Council) and the insurance documents
our public liability (£1m) and employers' liability (£10m) certificates." Log the procurement contact
and the renewal date of their framework.

### 7. "Send me your insurance certificates and driver vetting."
"Of course, I'll send them today." **Only if confirmed:** [CONFIRM: hire and reward
motor insurance and public liability, insurer and limits] and [CONFIRM: exact
driver licensing and DBS wording]. If they're not confirmed: "I'll get the
certificates over to you in writing from our office." Never describe cover you
haven't seen on paper.

### 8. "What are your payment terms?"
"We invoice monthly on account. Each journey is a line on one VAT invoice from
Central Executive Transfers Ltd. Terms are [CONFIRM: 30 days from invoice. That's
the Command Centre's default for new accounts]. You can pay by bank transfer
[CONFIRM bank details on invoice], or by card through a payment link."

### 9. "We need a PO number / cost centre on every invoice."
"That's fine. Every journey on the invoice carries the cost code or reference you
gave when booking, and we can make a cost code compulsory on your account so no
booking goes in without one. [CONFIRM: whether we can also print a single PO
number in the invoice header. If not, we'll put it in the reference for each
journey.]"

### 10. "What if the driver's late?"
"You'll know before your visitor does. You get the driver's details and a live
tracking link before pickup, and tracking starts when the driver sets off. Our
system alerts the office if a job isn't moving when it should be, and a director
answers the phone. If we're ever late, ring us and we'll sort it out."
[CONFIRM: any late-arrival policy, such as a refund or credit, before offering one.]

### 11. "What if the flight's delayed or cancelled?"
"We track the flight, so the driver works to the real landing time, not the
scheduled one. You don't need to ring us to move the pickup. [CONFIRM: free
waiting after landing at airports, and what happens if a flight is cancelled or
diverted.]"

### 12. "We need cars at short notice / early morning / late at night."
"Online booking needs at least 8 hours' notice. For shorter notice, ring
0114 399 8242 and we'll tell you straight away whether we can do it.
We take bookings and answer the phone 24/7, so short-notice work is fine.
Early starts are normal for airport work." Don't say "24/7" until it's confirmed.

### 13. "You're a small firm. What if your car breaks down or you're busy?"
"That's fair to ask. We're owner-run, and the two directors drive the executive
jobs. Larger vehicles can be provided by vetted partner drivers, and we'll always
tell you when a partner is covering. [CONFIRM: whether partner drivers also cover
executive saloon work when both directors are booked or a car is off the road.]
Being small means you deal with the people responsible, not a call centre."

### 14. "What about data protection? Our visitors are senior people."
"Calls and texts between your visitor and the driver go through a masked number,
so neither sees the other's real number. The tracking link shows only the
driver's first name, and it's only live while the job is under way. We use
passenger details only to do the job. [CONFIRM: whether we have a data processing
agreement or privacy notice to send, and the retention periods to quote.]"

### 15. "Can we just pay by card?"
"Yes. We can send a card payment link for each journey and issue a VAT invoice,
or you can use the account and pay monthly."

### 16. "Will it be the same driver each time?"
"For executive saloons it's usually one of the two directors. On a return trip we
keep the same driver for both legs wherever we can. If a visitor prefers a
particular driver, tell us and we'll do our best." Don't guarantee a named driver.

### 17. "We need a group moved / an event."
"We run the Mercedes V-Class (7 seats, 7 bags) and minibuses (7 seats and 8 seats).
Larger groups can be covered with vetted partner drivers. Give me the numbers,
times and luggage and I'll quote it as one price. [CONFIRM: the largest group we'll
take on, and whether we arrange coaches.]"

### 18. "Do you go to London / long distance?"
"Yes, UK-wide. For example, Barnsley to Heathrow is £320 in an executive saloon,
fixed. I'll quote your exact route." Check every figure in Pricing before quoting.

### 19. "Do you have electric or hybrid cars? We have a sustainability policy."
"[CONFIRM: fleet fuel types and any emissions data we can provide.] I'll send you
the exact vehicles we use." Don't claim green credentials until they're confirmed.

### 20. "Is there a contract or minimum term?"
"No minimum term [CONFIRM]. It's a rolling account on plain-English terms, and
either side can close it. I'll send the terms with the rate card."
(See `account-terms-DRAFT.md`, which isn't approved yet.)

### 21. "Can you sign our supplier code of conduct / modern slavery / anti-bribery forms?"
"Send them over and I'll go through them." [CONFIRM: which policies we hold in
writing, such as health and safety, equality, anti-bribery and modern slavery.]
Don't sign anything on the spot. A director reads it first.

### 22. "Call me in six months."
"Will do. Which month works best?" Log it as Not interested with the date in the
notes, and don't contact them before then.

### 23. "Not interested. Please take me off your list."
"Of course, sorry to have bothered you. I've removed you." Mark them **Do not
contact** the same day. No final pitch.
