# Maj's daily sales routine (45 to 60 minutes)

The aim is steady, not heroic: about 30 new conversations started a month, every
follow-up sent on the day it's due, nothing forgotten. If a job lands in the
window, drive the job. Sales waits until tomorrow. **Never write or send sales
emails from the phone while you're on a job.**

Reference files:
- First emails: `../2026-10-28-emails.md` (batch 1) and later batch files
- Follow-ups, LinkedIn, referral version: `follow-up-sequence.md`

## The rules that protect the mailbox

1. **Send from your real Microsoft 365 mailbox**, one message at a time, as
   yourself. No mail-merge tools, no bulk-send add-ins, no shared "sales@" address.
   [CONFIRM: which mailbox Maj sends from, e.g. maj@centralexecutivetransfers.co.uk
   or admin@, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC are set up for the domain in Microsoft 365.]
2. **Volume:** at least **10 new emails a week**, and no more than **20 sales emails
   a day** in total (new emails plus follow-ups). If follow-ups and new emails due
   come to more than 20, send the follow-ups first (they're warmer) and push the
   rest of the new emails to the next sending day.
3. **Plain text.** No images, no logo in the signature, no HTML templates, no
   colours. The signature is typed text.
4. **No attachments on a first email.** The rate card or the "Why CET" sheet goes
   only after they reply and ask for it. The first email should carry no links
   apart from the website in the signature.
5. **No tracking pixels and no read receipts.** Don't use "request a read receipt" in
   Outlook, and don't use any open-tracking tool. They hurt deliverability and they
   annoy PAs.
6. **Best time for new emails: Tuesday to Thursday, 08:00 to 10:00.** Follow-ups go
   out in the same morning window on the day they're due. If a due date falls on a
   weekend, send it on the next weekday.
7. **Every email has the opt-out line.** If anyone says no, stop, or unsubscribe, mark
   them **Do not contact** in Outreach the same day. That status is permanent.
8. **Watch for warning signs.** If more than 1 in 20 emails bounces in a week, or a
   prospect tells you your email went to junk, stop new sends for that week and tell
   Abdi. Check the addresses before sending more.

## The stop rule

**Three touches with no reply means we close politely.** The three touches are
email 1, the day-4 follow-up and the day-10 follow-up. Around day 18 you send the
close-the-loop email and then stop: set the prospect to closed (no reply) and
don't contact them again for six months.

The phone call and the LinkedIn connect run alongside those emails. They don't
extend the sequence. Each prospect gets at most one call (with one voicemail) and
one LinkedIn connect per sequence. If anyone replies at any point, the sequence
stops and you have a conversation instead.

## Before anyone is contacted

- **Check Outreach (or `PIPELINE.md`) first.** If the company is already there, check
  the status and the owner. Whoever sent email 1 owns all the follow-ups, so you
  don't email Abdi's prospects and he doesn't email yours.
- **Company addresses only.** Use a named role (PA to the MD, office manager,
  travel or facilities manager, procurement, HR) at a published business address.
  Don't guess personal addresses and don't email sole traders without consent.
- **Check partnerships.** A Ltd or an LLP is fine to email. A traditional partnership
  (some smaller accountancy and law firms) counts as an individual under PECR, so it
  needs consent first. Check Companies House if you're not sure.
- **Calls:** call published business numbers (the switchboard or the office line),
  never personal mobiles found online. Show your number, don't withhold it.
  [CONFIRM: how we screen numbers against the Corporate TPS before a cold
  follow-up call. Until that's agreed, only call prospects who have replied, given
  a number, or asked to be called.]

## The daily routine

### Tuesday to Thursday (sending days), 55 to 60 minutes

| Time | Minutes | What |
|---|---|---|
| 08:00 | 5 | **Inbox first.** Read replies. Answer anything warm straight away. Log replies and opt-outs in Outreach. |
| 08:05 | 10 | **Follow-ups due.** Open Outreach, go to the follow-ups due list, and send each one from `follow-up-sequence.md` with the first line personalised. Mark each as sent. |
| 08:15 | 25 | **New emails, up to 10.** Use the prepared draft, check the named contact and address, personalise the first line if needed, then send one at a time with a minute or two between them. Mark each as emailed. |
| 08:40 | 5 | **LinkedIn.** Send connection notes to anyone due one (after the day-10 email). Reply to anyone who accepted. |
| 08:45 | 5 | **Log and set next actions.** Every prospect touched today has a status and a next-action date. |
| 14:00 to 15:00 | 10 | **Three phone follow-ups** using `phone-script.md`. Mid-afternoon is usually the easiest time to reach a PA or office manager. Log each outcome. |

### Monday, 45 minutes

- 08:00 inbox and replies, as above (5 minutes).
- Follow-ups due, as above (10 minutes). These are fine to send on a Monday.
- **No new cold emails.** Instead, prepare the week (20 minutes): check the drafts
  for this week's new prospects, find the named contact for each, and confirm the
  company still fits (no closure, no takeover, no profit warning).
- Three phone follow-ups (10 minutes).

### Friday, 45 minutes

- Inbox, follow-ups due and calls, as above (25 minutes).
- **Weekly review (20 minutes, see below).** No new cold emails on Fridays.

## Logging in the Command Centre (Outreach page)

The Outreach page is being built. Until it's live on staging, log everything in
`docs/growth/sales/PIPELINE.md`. Once it's live, Outreach is the one record. Don't
keep a second list in a spreadsheet or a notebook.

How it works: you open the draft and click the email button, and it opens in your
own mailbox through a mailto link. The Command Centre never sends anything itself.
Then you mark it sent.

| You did | Mark it as | Next action date |
|---|---|---|
| Sent email 1 | Emailed | Set automatically, day 4 |
| Sent the day-4 follow-up | Follow-up 1 sent | Set automatically, day 10 |
| Sent the day-10 follow-up | Follow-up 2 sent | Set automatically; flagged to close on day 18 |
| Sent close-the-loop | Closed as no reply | Six months' time, in the notes |
| Call or voicemail | Add a **note**: "Call: spoke to reception, PA is Sarah, prefers email" | Leave as it is |
| LinkedIn connect or message | Add a **note**: "LinkedIn connect sent" | Leave as it is |
| They replied | Replied | Your reply within the same day |
| Call booked | Meeting booked | The call date |
| Rate card or proposal sent | Proposal sent | +3 working days to check it arrived |
| Account form received and approved | Account opened | Diary a referral ask after the first month |
| "Not now, try in spring" | Not interested, plus a note with the date | The date they gave |
| "No thanks" or "remove me" | **Do not contact** | None. This is permanent. |

Notes are for business facts only, for example who books, which routes, who
approves suppliers and when their budget renews. Don't write personal opinions
about people and don't paste in personal mobile numbers.

## Weekly review (Friday, 20 minutes; Abdi joins if he can)

Write these numbers at the top of `PIPELINE.md` (or read them off Outreach):

| Measure | This week | Target |
|---|---|---|
| New first emails sent | | 10 minimum, about 30 is ideal |
| Follow-ups sent on the day they were due | | 100% |
| Replies (any kind) | | |
| Calls made / calls where you spoke to someone | | 15 / 5 |
| Calls booked | | 1 to 2 |
| Rate cards or proposals sent | | |
| Accounts opened / partners live | | |
| Bounces / opt-outs | | Bounces under 1 in 20 |

Then answer four questions:
1. **What got replies?** Which sector, which opening line, which role. Do more of that.
2. **What got nothing?** If a sector has had 10 or more emails and no replies, change the angle or drop it.
3. **What's stuck?** Anything at "Proposal sent" for more than a week needs a call.
4. **Next week's list.** Ask the growth agent for the next 10 to 30 researched
   prospects, and agree who sends which so nobody is contacted twice.

Once a month, send the referral ask (`../referral-ask-account-clients.md`) to any
account that has been live for a month or more and hasn't been asked in the last
six months.
