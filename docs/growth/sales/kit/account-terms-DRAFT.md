## 3. Booking

- Book by phone (0114 399 8242), email (admin@centralexecutivetransfers.co.uk), or

  us and we'll confirm whether we can do it.

- If your account needs a cost code or PO reference, give it when you book. It
  will appear against that journey on your invoice. We can set your account so
  that no booking is accepted without one.

- Journeys are charged at the fixed prices on your agreed rate card, or at the

- We'll give you at least [CONFIRM: 30] days' written notice of any change to your

## 5. Waiting time

- Pickups from an address: the first [CONFIRM: 15] minutes are free, then waiting is
  charged at the rate-card rate, by the minute.
- Airport pickups: we track the flight and work to the actual landing time. Waiting
  is free for [CONFIRM] minutes after [CONFIRM: landing / on-stand], then charged at
  the rate-card rate.
- Extra stops: waiting at each stop is charged from arrival.
- Waiting charges appear as a separate line on the invoice.

## 6. Cancellations, changes and no-shows

- You can cancel free of charge up to [CONFIRM: 24] hours before the pickup time
  ([CONFIRM: 48] hours for London and the southern airports).
- Cancellations after that are charged at [CONFIRM]%. Cancellations once the driver

- **No-show:** if the passenger can't be found [CONFIRM] minutes after the pickup

- Changes to time or address are free if we can accommodate them. A change that
  makes the journey longer is re-priced, and we'll tell you the new price before
  the journey.

- We send **one VAT invoice per month** [CONFIRM: or another agreed period]

## 9. Queries and disputes

- Please raise any query about an invoice within [CONFIRM: 14] days of receiving

- Complaints about a journey go to a director: 0114 399 8242 or

## 10. Our service

- Executive saloons are normally driven by the directors. Some journeys, especially
  in larger vehicles, are carried out by vetted partner drivers. We'll tell you
  when that's the case.
- We send the driver's details and a live tracking link before each pickup.
  Calls and texts between passenger and driver go through a masked number.
- We'll supply the vehicle class you booked. We don't guarantee a particular make,
  model or colour.

## 11. Passengers

- Passengers must wear seatbelts and follow the driver's reasonable instructions.

- Child seats must be requested when booking.
- If a passenger damages or soils a vehicle, we'll charge the reasonable cost of
  cleaning or repair [CONFIRM: a fixed soiling charge, if any] and tell you first.
- The driver may refuse or end a journey if a passenger's behaviour puts safety at risk.

We'll keep items left in our vehicles for [CONFIRM: 28] days and arrange return

We use passenger and booker details only to carry out and invoice your journeys,
and we keep them secure in line with UK GDPR. We don't share them with anyone

## 15. Ending the account

Either side can close the account at any time with [CONFIRM: 30] days' written
notice. There's no minimum term [CONFIRM]. Journeys already completed are still
invoiced and payable on the normal terms. We can suspend an account straight away

  rate card, then these terms.
- Any change to these terms must be in writing from a director.
- These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales.
